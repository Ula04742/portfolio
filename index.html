About

I am an IT Operations and Automation Engineer with a hybrid background in systems integration, process design, and project delivery. My experience spans banking, e-commerce, and enterprise system integration, with a focus on workflow automation, operational efficiency, and cross-functional execution.

At E.SUN Bank Australia, I support IT operations across two branches and design workflow automation solutions using Power Automate, VBA, RPA, and M365. These solutions reduce repetitive manual work, strengthen operational continuity, and improve process reliability. I also contribute to system integration, SharePoint development, and BCP execution.

Previously, I managed large-scale O2O and system integration projects at momo, working with cross-functional teams, external vendors, and business stakeholders to deliver enterprise solutions from design through to implementation. My background also includes systems analysis, software support, and teaching assistance in multimedia and web-related environments.

I am committed to continuous technical and professional growth. I completed Stanford University’s Code in Place program, which strengthened my algorithmic thinking and automation-related problem-solving skills.
